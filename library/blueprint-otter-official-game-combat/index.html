RPG Combat Starter Blueprint

Connect actor creation, damage calculation, defeat conditions, and rewards.

ID: otter-official/game-combat Version: 1.0.0 License: MIT Framework: Otter >=1.0.1

Architecture & Runtime Preview

Otter >=1.0.1

Data flows from enemyInit and attack into game-entity.applyDamage. The remaining health is evaluated by a conditional defeat check, which activates victory reward output when health is at most 0.

  1. 1Spawn Enemy (pack-call: game-entity.createActor)
  2. 2Player Attack (input)
  3. 3Apply Combat Damage (pack-call: game-entity.applyDamage)
  4. 4Check Defeat Condition (condition)
  5. 5Grant Victory Reward (output)

templates/combat.ot.tmpl · otter 
# Generated by Otter Blueprint: otter-official/game-combat 1.0.0
# Standard editable Otter 1.0.1 source code

use "${import:otter-official/game-entity}"
use "${import:otter-official/math-utils}"

# Step 1: Spawn Opponent
enemyHp is ${startingHealth}
enemy is createActor "Dungeon Goblin" enemyHp 1
say "Enemy encountered:" name of enemy "with" health of enemy "HP"

# Step 2: Player Attack
attackDmg is ${baseAttackDamage}
say "Player attacks for" attackDmg "damage!"

# Step 3: Apply Combat Damage
remHp is applyDamage enemy attackDmg
say name of enemy "remaining HP:" remHp

# Step 4: Check Defeat Condition and Grant Reward
if remHp is at most 0
    say "Victory! The enemy has been defeated."
    say "Experience awarded: 100 XP"
otherwise
    say "The battle continues! Enemy is still standing."
.

otter blueprint explain otter-official/game-combat
otter blueprint apply otter-official/game-combat --project ./my-app

This blueprint sets up a game combat fight. It spawns an enemy with 100 health, deals 35 damage with an attack, checks if the enemy was defeated, and prints the result.
